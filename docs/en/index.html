Nùkún keeps an eye on the world.

All the research you care about, in your language. Every day, Nùkún gathers open-access papers and news from major research bodies on the subjects you pick. It translates them, explains the hard parts and learns from what you read.

Three minutes to get started.

  1. Say what interests you

    Pick from the catalogue or type any subject: “sleep”, “sewing”, “black holes”. Change them whenever you like.

  2. Add a free Google key

    It powers translations and explanations. You create it in one click on Google AI Studio, no credit card needed. The help page shows where to paste it.

  3. Read, and the feed adjusts

    Every article you open, save or dismiss tunes the next ones. Everything stays on your PC: your profile is sent nowhere.

Nùkún also finds work published in German, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese or Russian, and lets you read it in English.

Frequent questions

For everything else, the help page explains where each setting lives and what each message means.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. Translations go through Gemini with your own Google key, whose free quota is enough for daily use. You can also use a local AI (Ollama) or your Claude subscription if you have one.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

The app is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows flags it as a precaution. Click “More info” then “Run anyway”. The code is public on GitHub.

What happens to what I read?

Your reading profile, notes and library stay on your PC. Only the text to translate or explain is sent to the AI you chose.

What computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, 4 GB of memory and about 1 GB of free space. On first launch, the app downloads a 130 MB model used for recommendations.

What about updates?

When a new version is out, a small window offers it at the bottom of the screen. Nothing installs without your click, and you never need to reinstall.

Mac or Linux?

Not for now. Nùkún is built and tested on Windows.
