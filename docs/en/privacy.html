Privacy and legal notice

In short: Nùkún collects nothing about you. No account, no ads, no analytics.

The app and your data

Nùkún runs on your computer, without an account. Your reading profile, interests, library, notes, writing, translations and settings are stored only on your PC, in %APPDATA%\Nukun. The app's author has no access to them and receives no data, no usage statistics and no error reports.

Your API keys (Google, Claude, Semantic Scholar) also stay on your PC. They are only used to call the matching service.

To erase everything: uninstall the app, then delete the folder above.

Services the app uses

To work, the app contacts these services directly, from your computer:

  • Science and news sources (arXiv, Europe PMC, PLOS, OpenAlex, NASA, CNRS…): to find papers and download their text and images. They receive searches related to your interests, as when a browser visits their sites.
  • The AI you choose: Google Gemini with your key, Anthropic's Claude with your subscription or key, or Ollama locally, which sends nothing over the Internet. Only the passages to translate or explain, and your questions about the paper, are sent to them. Their use follows those services' terms, which you accept when creating your key or account with them.
  • Hugging Face: once, to download the recommendation model. It then runs on your PC.
  • GitHub: to find out whether a new version exists, and to download it when you click Update.

These services may see your IP address, as with any Internet connection.

This website

This site sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other sites: its fonts are hosted with it. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs, as explained in GitHub's privacy statement.

Papers and translations

Papers belong to their authors and publishers, under their own licences. Nùkún only shows open-access papers, on your computer and for your personal use. It always names the source and links to the official page. It does not get around any protection: a protected paper is not shown.

Translations, summaries and explanations are produced automatically by AI and may contain errors. They do not replace the original text and are not medical, legal or professional advice. For any decision, refer to the original paper and to a specialist.

What you write and export with the app is yours. If you publish it, respect the licences of the papers you cite.
